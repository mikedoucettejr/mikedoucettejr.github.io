## Tony Hawk Jumbotron & Music City Skate Jam — Event AV / Installation Work

**Role:** AV / Installation Technician & Video‑Switch Operator

**Event:** Music City Skate Jam (presented by Harley‑Davidson) featuring Tony Hawk skate demos and a live performance by Kip Moore, held at Music City Walk of Fame Park, Nashville, TN — September 10, 2017. <a href="https://www.thegamenashville.com/events/116697/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Event details</a>

### Responsibilities & Contributions

- Assisted in the setup and rigging of a custom jumbotron video‑display system for the event, including placement, structural support, and on‑site logistics.

- Operated the multi‑feed video switcher to manage live video streams for the jumbotron, coordinating feeds with camera operators and event crew to deliver synchronized video for a large audience.

- Contributed to event load‑in, strike, and transport, supporting both pre‑show build and post‑event teardown.

- Worked under time and coordination pressure to support a high‑visibility public event combining live sports, music, and large‑scale AV infrastructure.

### Event Context & References

The Music City Skate Jam was a combined skateboarding demonstration and live concert event — merging the skate culture of Tony Hawk with the country‑rock audience of Kip Moore. Proceeds benefited youth‑oriented foundations and hurricane‑relief efforts. <a href="https://tasteofcountry.com/kip-moore-tony-hawk-music-city-skate-jam/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Taste of Country</a>
