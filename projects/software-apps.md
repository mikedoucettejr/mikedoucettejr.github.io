## RADIO MP3

**Radio MP3** is a minimalist, screenless music device built on Raspberry Pi, designed to deliver curated, offline audio experiences. It emulates the feeling of turning on a radio station — no streaming, no algorithmic feeds, just handpicked music that refreshes quietly in the background. It's ideal for audiophiles, creatives, kids, and digital minimalists.

### Hardware + Software Stack

- **Device:** Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W  
- **Playback:** MPD or Python script for randomized MP3 shuffle  
- **Sync Methods:** USB, Wi-Fi (cron job), or SD card refresh  
- **Interface:** Optional screen/buttons for minimal tactile controls  
- **Enclosure:** 3D-printed or retro-fitted shells for analog feel  

### Content & Licensing Model

- **Curated Sets:** Weekly/monthly drops from DJs, artists, or creator  
- **Non-interactive Playback:** Enables potential performance licensing instead of mechanical/interactive rights  
- **Ideal Use Cases:** Safe music for kids, offline productivity zones, artist promo tool, anti-screen movement  

### Roadmap & IP

1. Alpha prototype: Fully offline playback with curated MP3 set  
2. Backend: Music sync with n8n or headless CMS  
3. Hardware refinement + beta units  
4. Explore IP protection: "Offline radio refresh device" concept  
5. Launch: Limited-run drop or direct artist/label integrations
